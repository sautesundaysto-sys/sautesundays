Moroccan cooking assumes you are feeding a lot of people and that everyone is staying a while. *Mourad: New Moroccan* by Mourad Lahlou suited us for exactly that reason.

![The Moroccan table at Sauté Sundays](/events/moroccan-may-2026/event1.jpg)

Most of what arrived had been cooking for hours. You could tell — the spicing had settled into everything rather than sitting on top of it.

![Dishes from the Moroccan table](/events/moroccan-may-2026/event2.jpg)

It was the first properly warm month of the year, which changed the mood of the room entirely.

![More from the May table](/events/moroccan-may-2026/event3.jpg)

The kind of table where people keep going back for a bit more of something rather than taking a full second plate.

![The table at Moroccan night](/events/moroccan-may-2026/event4.jpg)

Thank you to everyone who cooked for this one — it was a generous table in every sense.
