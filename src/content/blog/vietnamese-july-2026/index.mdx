After a run of long braises and heavy months, this one felt like opening a window.

![The Vietnamese table at Sauté Sundays](/events/vietnamese-july-2026/event1.jpg)

Vietnamese cooking runs on balance, and you notice it most when a dozen dishes land together — everything sharp, fresh and cut through with herbs.

![Dishes from the July table](/events/vietnamese-july-2026/event2.jpg)

There were more herbs on this table than we have had at any other night, by a wide margin.

![More from Vietnamese night](/events/vietnamese-july-2026/event3.jpg)

It was the right food for July, and the room stayed comfortable in a way that a heavier menu would not have allowed.

![The table at Vietnamese's Kitchen](/events/vietnamese-july-2026/event4.jpg)

Thank you to everyone who cooked. A very good way to spend a summer Sunday.
