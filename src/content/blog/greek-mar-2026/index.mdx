Greek cooking is deceptively simple on paper. Then you taste six versions of it side by side and realise how much room there is in a short ingredient list. This month's book was *My Big Fat Greek Cookbook*.

![The Greek table at Sauté Sundays](/events/greek-mar-2026/event1.jpg)

This was one of our fuller tables — a lot of dishes, all of them wanting to be eaten at the same time.

![Dishes from Greek night](/events/greek-mar-2026/event2.jpg)

I think this is also one of the favourites from our community that they actually bought the book after the event because how easy to recipes are to make really nice dishes.
![More from the March table](/events/greek-mar-2026/event3.jpg)

My favourite would be the orange cake that I did made it again for my birthday party.

![The table at Greek night](/events/greek-mar-2026/event4.jpg)

March is the month where Toronto starts to turn, and this felt like the first table of the year that was looking forward rather than hunkering down.

![More dishes from Greek night](/events/greek-mar-2026/event5.jpeg)

Thank you to everyone who cooked. A very good one.
