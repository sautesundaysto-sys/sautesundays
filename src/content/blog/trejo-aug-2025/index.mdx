Taco night is a format as much as a menu, and it turned out to be a very good fit for a cookbook club.

Cooking from Trejo's Tacos meant everyone arrived with one component rather than one finished plate — fillings, salsas, tortillas, the things you pile on top. Dinner assembled itself on the table, and people kept getting up to build another round.

Nobody stayed seated for long, which is usually the sign of a good one.

![Sauté Sundays August recap](/events/trejo-aug-2025/event1.png)

Thank you to everyone who cooked, and to everyone who kept the salsa moving down the table.
