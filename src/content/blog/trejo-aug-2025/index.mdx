*Trejo's Tacos* comes out of Danny Trejo's taquerias in Los Angeles, and it turned out to be built for the kind of night we run. Almost nothing in it is a single finished plate. It is components, and components are what a room of thirty five cooks does best.

So dinner assembled itself. Bana's barbacoa brisket anchored one end of the counter and my bacon cheeseburger taco the other, which is a pairing that should not work and did. Mady made the mushroom asada, which happened to be vegan, and Arjun made the mexi falafel, and both held their own against the meat. Meredith brought Mexican hot chocolate cookies and Montana brought a tray of Trejo's donuts, because somebody always reads to the back of the book.

![Sauté Sundays August recap](/events/trejo-aug-2025/event1.png)

Shout out to Bana, who spent 48 hours on that brisket. Impressed by the commitment. That is the sort of thing you only learn standing at the counter, when you compliment a dish and get told exactly how many days went into it.

Nobody stayed seated for long, which is usually the sign of a good one. Thank you to everyone who cooked, and to everyone who put that much effort in.
