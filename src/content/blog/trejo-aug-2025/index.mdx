*Trejo's Tacos* comes out of Danny Trejo's taquerias in Los Angeles, and it turned out to be built for the kind of night we run. Almost nothing in it is a single finished plate. It is components, and components are what a room of thirty five cooks does best.

So dinner assembled itself. The barbacoa brisket taco anchored one end of the counter and a bacon cheeseburger taco the other, which is a sentence that should not work and did. Between them sat a mushroom asada that happened to be vegan and a mexi falafel taco for the vegetarians, and both held their own against the meat. Mexican hot chocolate cookies and a tray of Trejo's donuts turned up for dessert, because somebody always reads to the back of the book.

![Sauté Sundays August recap](/events/trejo-aug-2025/event1.png)

Also shout out to the people who spent 48 hours making the brisket for our taco night. Impressed by the commitment. That is the sort of thing you only learn standing at the counter, when you compliment a dish and get told exactly how many days went into it.

Nobody stayed seated for long, which is usually the sign of a good one. Thank you to everyone who cooked, and to everyone who put that much effort in.
