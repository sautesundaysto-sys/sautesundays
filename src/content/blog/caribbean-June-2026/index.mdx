Caribbean night might have been our favourite one yet. It was the month the room clearly wanted before we announced it, and the sign ups went accordingly.

It was our biggest group at the table so far, and somehow every single dish landed. Jerk chicken, pepper shrimp, rice and peas, plantain in about four different forms. A tray of arroz con pollo sat in the middle and kept getting topped up. Somebody baked patties and was still lifting them off the sheet tray while people queued. We lost count of how many times someone went back for seconds.

![Caribbean Night table spread](/events/caribbean-june-2026/event1.jpeg)

The room was loud in the best way, and it felt like everyone had been waiting for an excuse to cook this cuisine. That is the difference between a month people sign up for and a month people arrive at with something they already knew how to make.

![Dishes at Caribbean Night](/events/caribbean-june-2026/event2.jpeg)

A big thank you to **Sunny Daze** for keeping us all cool with their lemonade all night. It disappeared fast.
