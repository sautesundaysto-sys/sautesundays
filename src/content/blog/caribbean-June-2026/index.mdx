Caribbean night might have been our favourite one yet. It was the month the room clearly wanted before we announced it, and the sign ups went accordingly.

It was our biggest group at the table so far, and somehow every single dish landed. Aditya made the jerk chicken and Rebecca the pepper shrimp. Mitch brought arroz con pollo, and Mady and Angie both made a pumpkin rice, entirely independently of each other, which is the risk you run without a sign up sheet and in this case nobody complained.

![Caribbean Night table spread](/events/caribbean-june-2026/event1.jpeg)

Then there was the plantain. Four people took plantain and went in four completely different directions: Greg made piñon, the plantain casserole, Vee did fried sweet plantains, Teresa fried green ones, and Marie made a plantain tarte tatin. That last one was my favourite thing all night. I did not know you could put plantain in a tart, and it came out sweet without being too sweet by Asian standards, which is a bar most desserts here do not clear.

![Dishes at Caribbean Night](/events/caribbean-june-2026/event2.jpeg)

The room was loud in the best way, and it felt like everyone had been waiting for an excuse to cook this cuisine. That is the difference between a month people sign up for and a month people arrive at with something they already knew how to make.

A big thank you to **Sunny Daze** for keeping us all cool with their lemonade all night. It disappeared fast.
