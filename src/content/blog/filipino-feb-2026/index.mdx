Filipino bar food is designed for exactly this — food that shows up in waves, meant to be picked at while everyone talks over each other. *Pulutan!* by Marvin Gapultos gave us a whole book of it.

It made for one of our loudest tables. Nothing arrived as a formal course, plates kept appearing, and the evening stretched well past when we thought it would end.

![The table at Sauté Sundays Filipino night](/events/filipino-feb-2026/event1.jpeg)

February is a hard month in Toronto, and a warm, noisy room full of food was a good answer to it.

Thank you to everyone who cooked and to everyone who stayed late.
