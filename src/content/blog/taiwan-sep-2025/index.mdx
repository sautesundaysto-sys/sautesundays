*Win Son Presents a Taiwanese American Cookbook* comes from the Brooklyn restaurant of the same name, and it is less a survey of Taiwanese cooking than a record of what two cooks actually make. That suited a table like ours, which runs on lots of small things all meant to be shared.

![Sauté Sundays September recap](/events/taiwan-sep-2025/event1.png)

Maria made the beef rolls, rolled and sliced and gone quickly. Ocean made the sloppy bao, which is the kind of thing that sounds like a novelty until you eat one. Susanne turned up with two dishes, the clams and the wood ear mushrooms under a handful of cilantro, and Leena brought the shrimp skewers, which did not last. Sara made the blueberry muffins, not Taiwanese in any sense and nobody objected.

Teresa made the soup and brought it all the way from Markham, which is about an hour with a pot on the back seat. That is the detail I remember from this one. Somebody drove across the region with soup so a room of people they had mostly never met could have a bowl of it.

This was the month it became obvious the format worked. Nine dishes from one book, no duplicates, and enough variety that you could eat from one end of the table to the other and still not try everything.
