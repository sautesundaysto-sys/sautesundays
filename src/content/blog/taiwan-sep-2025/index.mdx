Taiwanese food is built for a table like ours — lots of small things, all meant to be shared, none of them precious about it.

We cooked from *Win Son Presents a Taiwanese American Cookbook*, and the spread leaned right into that. Braises that had clearly been going all afternoon, snacks that disappeared the moment they landed, and the kind of food people describe by telling you where they first ate it. A good chunk of the evening turned into everyone comparing notes on night markets.

![Sauté Sundays September recap](/events/taiwan-sep-2025/event1.png)

Thank you to everyone who cooked for this one and brought a bit of that energy to the table.
