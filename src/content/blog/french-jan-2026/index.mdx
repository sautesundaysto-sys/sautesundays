We picked the most intimidating cookbook we have done so far, in the coldest month of the year, and it was exactly the right call.

*Mastering the Art of French Cooking* does not let you rush. Every recipe has a step where you either commit or you don't, and January is the one month where nobody minds spending a whole afternoon on dinner.

![The French table at Sauté Sundays](/events/french-jan-2026/event1.jpeg)

A lot of the conversation was technique — people comparing how long things took, what went wrong the first time, what they would do differently.

![Dishes from the January table](/events/french-jan-2026/event2.png)

Thank you to everyone who took this one on. It was a proper cold-weather table, and worth every hour it took.
