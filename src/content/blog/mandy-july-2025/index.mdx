We started with *Mandy's Gourmet Salads*, the book from the Wolfe sisters behind the Montreal salad bars. It is not a health book. It is a book about making a salad the most generous thing on the table, which is exactly the argument we wanted to test in July.

![Salads on the table at Sauté Sundays](/events/mandy-july-2025/event1.jpg)

The bowls arrived enormous. Mady, my cohost, made the peach and prosciutto salad that went down the middle and set the tone for everything after it, sweet and salty and far too easy to keep returning to. Madison made Mandy's chocolate chip cookies and brought them wrapped individually, a level of care nobody had asked for. The dressings did the heavy lifting all night. At one point four people were passing jars around trying to work out what was in each one.

It was also our first public event for Sauté Sundays. Words cannot express how grateful I am that people showed up. We had no track record, no photos to show anyone, nothing but an invitation and a cookbook, and a room of strangers still walked in carrying a bowl each.
