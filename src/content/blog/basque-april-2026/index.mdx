This was the month where most of the room was cooking something genuinely unfamiliar, which is usually when these nights are at their best.

![The Basque table at Sauté Sundays](/events/basque-april-2026/event1.jpg)

Basque food sits in its own category — not quite what people expect from Spanish cooking, and much more specific than that.

![Dishes from the Basque feast](/events/basque-april-2026/event2.jpg)

The pintxos went first, as small things always do.

![More from the April table](/events/basque-april-2026/event3.jpg)

What came after was slower and richer, and the table shifted gear with it.

![The table at A Basque Feast](/events/basque-april-2026/event4.jpg)

A lot of people said afterwards that they would not have cooked any of it on their own, which is more or less the entire point of the club.

![More dishes from the Basque feast](/events/basque-april-2026/event5.jpg)

Thank you to everyone who took a chance on an unfamiliar recipe for this one.
