*Basque* by José Pizarro is a book about a very specific corner of northern Spain, and it is not what most people picture when they hear Spanish cooking. No paella. A lot of peppers, tinned fish, cider and things served on bread. That made it the month where most of the room was cooking something genuinely unfamiliar, which is usually when these nights are at their best.

![The Basque table at Sauté Sundays](/events/basque-april-2026/event1.jpg)

Louise brought the sardines and Fran a tomato salad with olive oil, both of which are exactly as simple as they sound and completely dependent on doing them properly. Negar made an aubergine, honey and blue cheese omelette, which is the sort of combination you would never assemble yourself and immediately want the recipe for. Marie made spinach and goat's cheese croquetas. There were toasts with requesón, red peppers and thyme that went before some people had taken their coats off.

![Dishes from the Basque feast](/events/basque-april-2026/event2.jpg)

The flavour of the night was Silkan's cream cheese ice cream with blackcurrant and camomile syrup, and I can only report on it secondhand, because by the time I got there it had gone. I stood and drank what had melted at the bottom. Worth it.

![More from the April table](/events/basque-april-2026/event3.jpg)

It was also the first time we did a collab with Danu Social House. Huge thanks for letting us run this community event there for free. Doing it in a room that is not ours, surrounded by their graffitied walls, changed the feel of the night in a way we did not expect.

![The table at A Basque Feast](/events/basque-april-2026/event4.jpg)

Another night packed with people and dishes, where I did not get a chance to try every single dish people made. A lot of people said afterwards that they would not have cooked any of it on their own, which is more or less the entire point of the club.
