*Basque* by José Pizarro is a book about a very specific corner of northern Spain, and it is not what most people picture when they hear Spanish cooking. No paella. A lot of peppers, salt cod, cider and things served on bread. That made it the month where most of the room was cooking something genuinely unfamiliar, which is usually when these nights are at their best.

![The Basque table at Sauté Sundays](/events/basque-april-2026/event1.jpg)

Pintxos led the way, as small things always do. Toasts topped with requesón, red peppers and thyme came out on a plate and were gone before some people had taken their coats off. What followed was slower and richer, and the table shifted gear with it.

![Dishes from the Basque feast](/events/basque-april-2026/event2.jpg)

It was also the first time we did a collab with Danu Social House. Huge thanks for letting us run this community event there for free. Doing it in a room that is not ours, surrounded by their graffitied walls, changed the feel of the night in a way we did not expect.

![More from the April table](/events/basque-april-2026/event3.jpg)

Another night packed with people and dishes, where I did not get a chance to try every single dish people made. That is a good problem and also a genuinely annoying one.

![The table at A Basque Feast](/events/basque-april-2026/event4.jpg)

A lot of people said afterwards that they would not have cooked any of it on their own, which is more or less the entire point of the club. Thank you to everyone who took a chance on an unfamiliar recipe.
